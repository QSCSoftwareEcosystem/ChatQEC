## Layout Description

### Qubit Count and Roles

The $d=3$ rotated surface code uses **24 qubits total** split into two roles:

| Role | Count | Description |
|------|-------|-------------|
| **Data qubits** (q0–q13) | 14 | Carry the encoded logical information; initialized in $\lvert 0\rangle$ at the start and measured in $Z$ at the end |
| **Measure (ancilla) qubits** (q14–q23) | 10 | Syndrome extraction ancillae; reset and measured every round |

The code encodes $k=1$ logical qubit with distance $d=3$ [^1].

---

### Stabilizer Structure

The 10 measure qubits correspond to the code's stabilizer generators — a mix of $X$- and $Z$-type checks [^1]:

- **$Z$-type (plaquette) ancillae** — q14, q16, q17, q20, q21: These couple to data qubits via **CNOT gates with the ancilla as *control***, accumulating $Z$-parity. No Hadamard is applied before/after, so they measure $\prod Z$.
- **$X$-type (vertex) ancillae** — q15, q18, q19, q22, q23: These use **Hadamard gates sandwiching the CNOTs** ($H \to \text{CNOT} \to H$), effectively measuring $\prod X$ on their neighboring data qubits.

This is the canonical CSS structure: $\bar{X}$ errors are detected by $Z$-stabilizers and vice versa [^1].

---

### Timeline Structure (one round, repeated ×9)

Each of the 9 rounds follows this sequence:

1. **`R`** — Reset all 10 ancilla qubits to $\lvert 0 \rangle$
2. **`H`** — Hadamard on the 5 $X$-type ancillae (basis change into $X$-measurement)
3. **`CX`** — Two layers of controlled-NOT gates coupling ancillae to their neighboring data qubits
4. **`H`** — Hadamard again on the 5 $X$-type ancillae (basis change back)
5. **`M`** — Measure all 10 ancillae in $Z$; outcomes feed **`DETECTOR`** annotations for syndrome tracking

---

### Final Measurement and Observable

After all 9 rounds, all 14 **data qubits** are measured in $Z$. The **logical $\bar{Z}$ observable** is declared as the product of 7 specific data-qubit measurements (a $Z$-string spanning the lattice from one smooth boundary to the other) [^1].
